Juggle frame advantage

Juggle frame advantage is frame advantage against an opponent who is still in the air: how many frames you can act before a launched opponent lands. For every move that launches, on a normal hit, a counter hit and a punish counter, this page gives it along with how long the opponent stays airborne, when you can act, their height on every frame, and how fast they drift away.

How it works

  • Airtime starts when the opponent's hitstop ends. The launch is a fixed arc read from the hit's reaction data: rising speed and gravity both come from its height and duration, so the same launcher gives the same arc against every character (checked on Ryu, C. Viper and Zangief).
  • The opponent drifts away at a constant speed for the whole time they are airborne (the reaction's distance divided by its duration).
  • They stay hittable the whole time they are in the air: the falling reactions keep a body hurtbox until the knockdown.
  • Juggle count: a launcher sets it; a follow-up only connects if its own juggle limit is at least the current count, and then adds to it.
  • Juggle frame advantage of 0 or less means the opponent lands before the attacker can act.
  • Whether a particular follow-up reaches also depends on its hitbox and travel; this page gives the timing, height, distance and juggle count to check it against.

Confirmed in Training: Ryu's H Hashogeki (normal hit: airborne 41, Ryu can act on frame 21, peak 105.3 cm, drift 3.42 cm per frame, every frame's height exact), L Hashogeki on counter hit and punish counter (exact). One open difference: a hit that lands on an already airborne opponent (Yasmine's H Lipad ng Agila) measured one frame longer than computed.
