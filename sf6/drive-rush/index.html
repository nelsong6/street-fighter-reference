Drive Rush momentum

A normal's Drive Rush momentum is how that normal moves when it is done out of Drive Rush, on top of its own movement. Two things combine: when you cancel (each character's rush has its own speed curve) and which normal you choose (each normal has its own multiplier, slowdown and stop frame).

How it works

  • Starting speed = the rush's speed on the frame you cancel × the normal's multiplier (most normals 1.0; Blanka's mostly 0.7).
  • Each frame the normal loses its slowdown (usually 0.5 cm per frame) until the speed reaches 0 or its stop frame cuts it off.
  • A few normals set their own fixed speed and ignore the rush (Alex's 5HP always starts at 8).
  • Specials carry no momentum.
  • While you push the opponent, both of you move at half speed. The slide pauses during hitstop and continues after it.
  • Separately, Drive Rush normals add 4 frames of hitstun and blockstun (a constant in the game's code).

The typical Drive Rush is the cast's median rush speed on each frame, with the most common normal settings (multiplier 1.0, slowdown 0.5) and the chosen normal's own stop frame and first active frame. Not shown: each normal's own animation movement, and pushing. Normals done from stances appear with their stance input (for example Alex's Prowler Stance follow-ups). Measured in Training on Alex (5MP from two cancel points, 2MK, 5HP, 6MP) and Blanka (5MP at three cancel points); every frame matched.
