Drive Rush momentum

A normal's Drive Rush momentum is how that normal moves when it is done out of Drive Rush, on top of its own movement. It comes from two separate things:

  1. The character's Drive Rush speed. Each character has one rush speed curve, the same for all of their normals. The speed you carry in depends only on the character and the frame you cancel on.
  2. The normal's own settings. Each normal has a multiplier (how much of the rush speed it keeps), a slowdown (how much speed it loses each frame) and a stop frame (where the slide is cut off). A few normals ignore the rush and set their own speed.

How it works

  • Starting speed = the rush's speed on the frame you cancel × the normal's multiplier (most normals 1.0; Blanka's mostly 0.7).
  • Each frame the normal loses its slowdown (usually 0.5 cm per frame) until the speed reaches 0 or its stop frame cuts it off.
  • A few normals set their own fixed speed and ignore the rush (Alex's 5HP always starts at 8).
  • Specials carry no momentum.
  • While you push the opponent, both of you move at half speed. The slide pauses during hitstop and continues after it.
  • Separately, Drive Rush normals add 4 frames of hitstun and blockstun (a constant in the game's code).

Timing

Every character's rush runs on the same clock: a screen freeze of 11 frames out of a parry (10 when Drive Rush is cancelled out of a normal), then 7 more rush frames before you can cancel it into an attack (any normal, special or super), on rush frame 18 at the earliest and until rush frame 55. The speeds before that point only move you; they are never carried into anything. Only normals carry the speed further; specials and supers start without it.

Moving is different: you cannot jump, crouch, walk or block out of the rush before rush frame 31. From rush frame 31, holding any direction (forward included) stops the rush in 2 frames and you move that way. With no direction held the rush runs to rush frame 55, and if you are still holding the parry buttons it turns into a Drive Parry there (Ryu, measured in Training: up, down, back and forward each stopped the rush after rush frame 30; nothing held ran to 55; parry held went into Drive Parry at 55).

So for a late cancel, keep the stick neutral and press the attack when you want it; the parry buttons can stay held or not. A direction pressed together with the attack's button is fine: 2MK and 6MP pressed on rush frame 33 came out as normal, and so did 5HP with MP+MK still held, and 5MP after letting go of MP for one frame and pressing it again.

Worked example, the fastest Drive Rush jab (Ryu, measured in Training): 3 frames of parry while 66 is input, the 11-frame freeze (not counted), 7 rush frames, then the jab's 4 frames of startup: 14 frames from the parry press to the jab's first active frame, 25 on screen including the freeze.

The typical Drive Rush is the cast's median rush speed on each frame, with the most common normal settings (multiplier 1.0, slowdown 0.5) and the chosen normal's own stop frame and first active frame.
